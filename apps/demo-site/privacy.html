DataHaven Technologies Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

1. Overview

At DataHaven, we respect your privacy while providing world-class enterprise cloud services. This document outlines how we collect, process, and store your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, we collect compulsory credentials (Name, Email, Password) as well as optional profile attributes you provide (Phone Number, Date of Birth, Office Address, Job Title, Annual Budget, Tax ID).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate our cloud services, authenticate your access, issue invoices, and communicate system status.

4. Marketing & Promotional Communications

Section 4.1, Marketing Opt-In: By filling out optional fields (such as Phone Number, Job Title, or Annual Budget), you consent to receiving promotional email campaigns, SMS updates, and telemarketing calls from DataHaven and our authorized promotional partners.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

Section 5.2, Partner Sharing: We may share non-aggregated personal identifiers (including email, job title, and company size) with third-party advertising platforms, CRM providers, and commercial analytics vendors to serve tailored enterprise advertisements.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active, plus an additional 7 years to comply with statutory accounting regulations.

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal data at any time. To submit a data erasure request, visit our official Account Deletion & Data Rights Portal or contact privacy@datahaven-demo.com.
